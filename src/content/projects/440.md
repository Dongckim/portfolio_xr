**EST 440 Interdisciplinary Research Project (Team 3)**. This project transitions PTSD care from static, co-located models to a **"Therapy as Software" (TaaS)** paradigm. By integrating neuroscientific principles with immersive technology, we developed a prototype that delivers **Parametric Exposure**—allowing clinicians to control the intensity, proximity, and duration of trauma-relevant cues in a safe, repeatable environment.

### **The 3-Stage Exposure Protocol**

The system uses a graded exposure hierarchy to safely move patients through trauma-relevant triggers while preventing re-traumatization.

| **Stage 1: Sound Exposure** | **Stage 2: Audio-Visual** | **Stage 3: Field Hospital** |

### **Technical Implementation**

#### 1. Adaptive & Closed-Loop Architecture
The core intelligence of the platform is an **Adaptive Algorithm** that fuses subjective distress reports with objective biometric data.
* **Biometric Integration**: Real-time APIs pull heart rate variability (HRV) and arousal indicators from sensors to keep patients within the "therapeutic window".
* **Trigger Control API**: Provides precise modulation of sound, light, motion, and proximity gradients to ensure exposure updates threat models without escaping safety control.

#### 2. Safety Systems: The "Kill Switch"

Exposure therapy involves risks such as dissociation or overwhelming distress. To mitigate this, we engineered a dedicated safety layer.
* **WebSocket Override**: A Node.js server facilitates real-time, low-latency communication between the clinician's dashboard and the headset.
* **Immediate Cessation**: The clinician can trigger a "hard stop" to terminate the simulation instantly if physiological or self-reported distress exceeds safety thresholds.

#### 3. UX & Interaction Design
* **Hand Tracking**: Using Meta Quest's 26-joint anchor tracking per hand, we eliminated controllers to increase immersion and reduce disruption.
* **Gestural UI**: Patients use a simple "clapping" gesture to navigate stages, which is intuitive and maintains the therapeutic state.
* **Self-Assessment**: Every stage concludes with a VR-integrated anxiety prompt (0-10 scale) to track subjective units of distress (SUDs) for clinical review.
